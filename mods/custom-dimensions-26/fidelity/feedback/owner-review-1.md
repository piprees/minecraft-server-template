# Owner review notes: Batches 1 and 2

The owner's notes from the two review pages, verbatim, with the rulings drawn from them. They bind every later package.

## Rulings

- A **place** is somewhere people would name: a fortress, a bastion, a pit full of bones, a huge lava waterfall, a crashed ship, a camp. Terrain and biome regions are the setting, not the place. Shots labelled as places must show one.
- **Structures** are essential to every dimension: the pack's structure mods where a description fits them, and our own set pieces for the stories the descriptions tell.
- A dimension's own **fog** holds across the whole dimension unless there is a good reason not to (`atmosphere.overBiomes` by default).
- **Structures never float in the void** under a sky world's islands.
- the_violet_spire is **night-only**, with crystals and growth on the spire tops.
- the_end: the central island stays classic; the **outer islands** carry the variety.
- End cities: a denser dungeons group; elytra at 8% a chest in End dungeon chests.
