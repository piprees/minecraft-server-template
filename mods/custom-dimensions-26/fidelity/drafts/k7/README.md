# K7 drafts: places for Batches 2–6

Each draft is its base with `biomeLayout.places` added: the k1, k2 or k3 draft where one exists (its top-level keys over `definitions/<slug>.json`), else the definition. Apply one by copying it over `definitions/<slug>.json`; it replaces the earlier draft for that slug. Where a draft also re-draws `biomeLayout.weights`, the row says so: a place's share is its weight, so the weights come from the description's "Mostly" and "Rarely". Three drafts touch `biomes`: the_fractured_halls (order), the_lantern_pools (one full-range box dropped), the_endless_expanse (a list added).

Kinds. **Meeting**: a few named places. **One place with a fringe**: one biome holds 50–55% of the border as one region, which the one-place target (`landBiomeRegions.largestPercent` ≥ 50) asks for and noise regions do not give (W9g baselines on the intent cards: largest 6–29%); the rest of the list makes two to four fringe places. **One place**, **deliberately broken**, **noise**: no draft, for the reason in the row.

Figures. Shares are the compiled place field sampled on a 128-cell grid over the smaller of the player and generation borders, with the unit tests' stand-in warp noise at seed 20261022 (`/tmp/claude-0/k7/dbg/`, K7Drafts): the server's noise moves the edges, not the shares. In every draft each place is one region of 1% or more and specks are 0.00%; every `neighbours` and `apart` pair held, and every `at` site and `core` centre lies inside its own place by at least a tenth of the border's radius (the compiler holds it or refuses). The full set (83 definitions, 35 drafts in place) compiles in 3.7 s. A group's biomes mix inside its place on humidity, and count as separate biome regions in the survey. Survey each with `/customdim survey <slug> 1 1`: read `placeness.landBiomeRegions` and `biomesPercent`, and on sky worlds `islands.dominantBiomePercent`.

Gaps the descriptions show and the layout cannot express: `/tmp/claude-0/k7/kit-gaps.md` (places by height). A small heart is `core` with the heart first in the biome list: a disc of its share at the centre, the others in wedges round it.
