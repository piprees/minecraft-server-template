# K6 drafts: places by construction

Each draft is its `definitions/` file with `biomeLayout.places` added and nothing else (the overworld also takes the biome list places need, below). The full set compiles with all five in place. Survey each (`/customdim survey <slug> 1 1`) and read `placeness.biomeRegions` and, on the sky worlds, `placeness.islands`. The place field figures below are the compiled field sampled on a 128-cell grid over the border with the unit tests' stand-in warp noise at seed 20261022; the server's own noise moves the edges, not the shares.

## the_aviary.json

Intent card: "a meeting of a few skyland regions". Thirteen regional biomes on a climate stretched four times read as thirteen noise blobs (top three 30.7% in the W10c1 pass). The draft makes five places of them by `groups`, each place's biomes mixed on the stretched humidity: `highlands` (sky highlands, snowy slopes, windswept isles; weights 28), `wisteria` (wisteria grove, cherry grove; 42), `golden` (golden skygrove, flower forest; 20), `meadows` (meadow, plains, cloud reef; 22), `woods` (forest, birch, dark forest; 15), with `neighbours` wisteria–golden so the two flowering places meet. Arrangement automatic (the seed picks among 32 layouts) over the 2,048-block generation border; the head's `climateWarp` (160 blocks over 512) is the dial, capped for places at 76 blocks (0.15 of the wavelength; the report says so). Field: 5 major regions, shares 22/33/16/17/12%, top three 72%. Should move `islands.dominantBiomePercent` up (an island lies in one place unless an edge crosses it; within a place its group still mixes), keep `landBiomeRegions.specksPercent` low and end the straight north–south boundary: place edges are a diagram's, warped. Read the group members' shares in `biomesPercent` together as each place's.

## overworld.json

Intent card: "a varied overworld read as broad regions, not a scatter"; targets `landBiomeRegions.top3Percent` ≥ 25 and the plains-and-forest family ≥ 20%. The head definition has no biome list, so it lays the vanilla preset's own; places need one. The draft lists 17 land biomes, six oceans and four shore and river biomes with five places: `plains` (plains, sunflower plains, meadow), `woods` (forest, birch, flower, dark forest), `north` (taiga, snowy taiga, snowy plains, grove, with the cold and frozen oceans, snowy beach and frozen river, which take the north's column of their role areas), `south` (savanna, desert, badlands, with the lukewarm and warm oceans) and `wetlands` (swamp, jungle, mangrove swamp); ocean, deep ocean, beach and river take every other place's sea and shore. `neighbours` north–woods and `apart` north–south keep snow away from desert. Shares 25/22.5/20/20/12.5%. Over the 8,192-block border each place is several thousand blocks across; the survey's inner 4,096 sees two to four of them. Should move `landBiomeRegions.top3Percent` well past 25 and `perKm2` far under 6; the plains family is a quarter of the land by construction. Peaks keep no biome of their own (the vanilla mountain biomes are not listed): mountains take their place's biome. Judge the cold share on its seed: it is 20% of the border, wherever the seed puts it. The head's `climateShift` acts on the temperature the places replace, so it changes nothing here, and `scales.climate` 4 now stretches only the humidity that mixes each place's biomes.

## the_sunken_temple.json

Intent card: "skyland highlands at spawn looking over water pooled between islands"; jungle on the islands, warm shallow basins. Two places: `highlands` (sky highlands and meadow, 25) held at the centre by `at` [0, 0], and `jungle_coast` (jungle and warm ocean, 25) placed automatically round it; the warm ocean takes the jungle's basins, the lukewarm and deep lukewarm oceans the highlands' (and every column's basins otherwise), hanging gardens the undersides everywhere. Field: 2 major regions, 51/49%. Should move `landBiomeRegions.top3Percent` from 14 towards the land's whole share, put spawn on highland ground (`at.spawn`), and show `minecraft:warm_ocean`, which the head layout lists but never placed, on the jungle's side.
