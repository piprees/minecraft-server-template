# K6 drafts: places by construction

Each draft is its `definitions/` file with `biomeLayout.places` added and nothing else (the overworld also takes the biome list places need, below). The full set compiles with all five in place. Survey each (`/customdim survey <slug> 1 1`) and read `placeness.biomeRegions` and, on the sky worlds, `placeness.islands`. The place field figures below are the compiled field sampled on a 128-cell grid over the border with the unit tests' stand-in warp noise at seed 20261022; the server's own noise moves the edges, not the shares.

## the_aviary.json

Intent card: "a meeting of a few skyland regions". Thirteen regional biomes on a climate stretched four times read as thirteen noise blobs (top three 30.7% in the W10c1 pass). The draft makes five places of them by `groups`, each place's biomes mixed on the stretched humidity: `highlands` (sky highlands, snowy slopes, windswept isles; weights 28), `wisteria` (wisteria grove, cherry grove; 32), `golden` (golden skygrove, flower forest; 20), `meadows` (meadow, plains, cloud reef; 22), `woods` (forest, birch, dark forest; 15), with `neighbours` wisteria–golden so the two flowering places meet. Arrangement automatic (the seed picks among 32 layouts), warp the default 102 blocks over 1,024 over the 2,048-block generation border. Field: 5 major regions, shares 23/26/18/19/14%, top three 68.5%. Should move `islands.dominantBiomePercent` up (an island lies in one place unless an edge crosses it; within a place its group still mixes), keep `landBiomeRegions.specksPercent` low and end the straight north–south boundary: place edges are a diagram's, warped. Read the group members' shares in `biomesPercent` together as each place's.

## the_nether.json

Intent card: "a meeting of the Nether's biomes", piglin heartland. `places: {}`: each of the six biomes is one place at its weight (wastes 28, crimson 25, warped 15, soul sand 15, basalt 10, quartz flats 8), arrangement automatic, warp 51 blocks over 512. Field: 6 major regions, shares 27/25/15/15/10/9%, top three 67%. Should move `landBiomeRegions.major` from 10 to 6 (the meeting rule's bound is 8) and `top3Percent` to about 68 (target 60), whatever the climate scale. The Nether's 3D biomes read the same field at every height, so a cave under one place is that place's biome. If the heartland should be central, the next try is `"arrangement": "core"` with `minecraft:crimson_forest` moved to the head of the list (a list change, not made here).

## overworld.json

Intent card: "a varied overworld read as broad regions, not a scatter"; targets `landBiomeRegions.top3Percent` ≥ 25 and the plains-and-forest family ≥ 20%. The head definition has no biome list, so it lays the vanilla preset's own; places need one. The draft lists 17 land biomes, six oceans and four shore and river biomes with five places: `plains` (plains, sunflower plains, meadow), `woods` (forest, birch, flower, dark forest), `north` (taiga, snowy taiga, snowy plains, grove, with the cold and frozen oceans, snowy beach and frozen river, which take the north's column of their role areas), `south` (savanna, desert, badlands, with the lukewarm and warm oceans) and `wetlands` (swamp, jungle, mangrove swamp); ocean, deep ocean, beach and river take every other place's sea and shore. `neighbours` north–woods and `apart` north–south keep snow away from desert. Shares 25/22.5/20/20/12.5%. Over the 8,192-block border each place is several thousand blocks across; the survey's inner 4,096 sees two to four of them. Should move `landBiomeRegions.top3Percent` well past 25 and `perKm2` far under 6; the plains family is a quarter of the land by construction. Peaks keep no biome of their own (the vanilla mountain biomes are not listed): mountains take their place's biome. Judge the cold share on its seed: it is 20% of the border, wherever the seed puts it.

## the_violet_spire.json

Intent card: "a place where two worlds overlapped", wisteria islands and violet spires, crystal and jungle patches as zones. Three places in `bands` running west to east (`direction` 90): `wisteria` (wisteria grove, lavender fields; 40 of 80), `skylands` (sky highlands, cherry grove; 25), `endward` (umbrella jungle, chorus forest; 15): the natural skylands meeting the End-coloured side across the middle band. Warp 34 blocks over 342 on the 683-block border. Field: 3 major regions, shares 49/32/19%. The two zones wrap the field as before (the router's temperature is the zones' channel over `places/the_violet_spire/place`). Should move `landBiomeRegions.major` from 11–15 to the places' islands within three bands (on islets the count reads islands; see placeness.md § Biome regions, Places), `top3Percent` up, and `islands.dominantBiomePercent` to near 100 except on islands a band edge crosses.

## the_sunken_temple.json

Intent card: "skyland highlands at spawn looking over water pooled between islands"; jungle on the islands, warm shallow basins. Two places: `highlands` (sky highlands and meadow, 25) held at the centre by `at` [0, 0], and `jungle_coast` (jungle and warm ocean, 25) placed automatically round it; the warm ocean takes the jungle's basins, the lukewarm and deep lukewarm oceans the highlands' (and every column's basins otherwise), hanging gardens the undersides everywhere. Field: 2 major regions, 51/49%. Should move `landBiomeRegions.top3Percent` from 14 towards the land's whole share, put spawn on highland ground (`at.spawn`), and show `minecraft:warm_ocean`, which the head layout lists but never placed, on the jungle's side.
