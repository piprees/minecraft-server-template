## The finding that shaped the design
A convex cell clear of a disc of radius m round the centre holds at most (1 − m)/2 of the square. So no power diagram keeps a small place at the centre beside a neighbour of 45% or more, whatever the solver does (moving free sites outwards, sectors rings, capacity-constrained Lloyd were all tried and fail on 20/50/15/15). The K7 cases (neighbours of 50, 51, 52, 55%) are impossible with convex cells.

## Design as built
- `PlaceField.PIN_MARGIN` = 0.1 border units: a pinned point must lie that far inside its place. `reach(a, i, u, v, margin)` measures it exactly from the power bisectors; `unheld(a, anchors)`.
- `hold(a, target, anchors, grid)`: for `at` pins, if a pin does not hold, a capacity-constrained Lloyd iteration (pinned site moves by centroid→pin gap, free sites half-way to centroid, weights re-solved, 30 rounds). Used in the auto search (`search`) and after `refine`, and for all-`at` definitions. Returns `a` unchanged when every pin holds (so goldens do not move).
- Heart: `Arrangement` gains `heart` (radius), `wedges` (compass bearings) and `hub` (the heart's place index). The hub place is the disc of area = its share (r = √(4 share/π)) round its point; the others are angular wedges from that point clockwise in list order, the first centred on `direction`, bearings integrated (`wedges(...)`, 36,000 steps, reach from the point to the square edge) so each wedge holds its share of the square less the disc. Every wedge meets the heart and reaches the border.
- DF encoding (compile `least`): heart term `floor(clamp(|p−c|²/r², 0, 1)) × (2^24 − 16) (+ hub)`, wedge term `floor(clamp(−m × 2^20, 0, 1)) × (2^24 − 16) + place`, m = min (span ≤ 180°) or max of the signed distances to the two rays. Node types are the existing ones (add, mul, square, clamp, floor, min, max): T217 shape unchanged in kind, only new constants/terms; no new boot check.
- Where it applies (PlaceLayout.build): `core` solves its even ring first and keeps it when the centre holds (every core whose first place is its largest: places_core golden unchanged); otherwise `PlaceField.heart(shares, 0, 0, 0, direction)`, refused with "the core arrangement cannot keep X (s%) at the centre with N blocks round it…" when the heart is narrower than the margin or one region check fails. `ring`, `sectors`, `bands` sites are not pinned. All-`at`: Lloyd hold, else refused naming the place and the place that reaches over it. Auto with `at` pins: search+hold; if nothing holds and exactly ONE place is pinned, that place becomes a heart at its site (hub ≠ 0 possible) — this is the fix in progress; else refused.

## Land shares write-up (notes, not yet measured)
- Precedent: `customdimensions:land_share` (server/.../zone/LandShareFunction) measures the seed's own noise when the router compiles (96×96 grid) and shifts continentalness. A place field could do the same: a custom DF type that, at compileSampler, samples the terrain's land cue (`<prefix>/place` > 0, the router's continents on void terrains) on a grid and re-solves the weights against the land mask. Roller and server agree because both compile the router for the seed through the same code. Cost per rolled seed: ~16k cue samples plus a weight solve (n × 16k × ≤400), roughly 0.1–0.2 s. Downsides: a new DF type (T217 shape change and a boot check), weights no longer in the datapack, a step-function objective on sparse islands (an edge through an island splits it; tolerance must follow island granularity), and the 4 × 8 seed-chosen layouts re-solved per seed.
- Cheaper compile-time alternative: solve against the expected land density (the islands noise's known spread plus `terrain.layout`'s deterministic shape, via Noises.threshold), seed-free, which removes the systematic bias of `radial` clusters (most of a cell in empty sky) but not per-seed scatter.
- Candidate users: the_aviary, the_sunken_temple, the_violet_spire, the_burning_archipelago (End coverage 0.04: few islands, scatter dominates), the_shattered_skies. K7's recipe for a radial cluster remains `sectors`. Measure the gap with the land harness before deciding.
