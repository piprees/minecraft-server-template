# the_molten_flats: the owner's note

The owner (change): I think this place could be even flatter, with less debris on the fields. We could de-emphasise the magma and instead have pockets of it, using black stone and obsidian to box the magma in. Bubbles of lava could be present still in the centres of the larger magma puddles, like its cooling off. It should have structures here that feel like industry, chains, levers, pipes, chimneys; they cleared this place, strip mined it, then built on the polluted land only to run out of space and quench the lava to build on that instead. Long since abandoned and monsters have moved in. They produced weapons and armour for the wars here; it should be sparse but some rough structures more like buildings/forts should remain, old barracks and shipping areas with broken portals, attempts at end-gateways and implied science, imprisoned witches and endermen, stacks of pearls and potions

## The owner's rulings for every dimension

- A **place** is somewhere people would name: a fortress, a bastion, a pit full of bones, a huge lava waterfall, a crashed ship, a camp. Terrain and biome regions are the setting, not the place. Shots labelled as places must show one.
- **Structures** are essential to every dimension: the pack's structure mods where a description fits them, and our own set pieces for the stories the descriptions tell.
- A dimension's own **fog** holds across the whole dimension unless there is a good reason not to (`atmosphere.overBiomes` by default).
- **Structures never float in the void** under a sky world's islands.
- the_violet_spire is **night-only**, with crystals and growth on the spire tops.
- the_end: the central island stays classic; the **outer islands** carry the variety.
- End cities: a denser dungeons group; elytra at 8% a chest in End dungeon chests.
