# Decisions only the owner can make

Each one changes the size of the remaining work (gaps.md) or what "done" means.

1. **Content before or after the switch.** The plan launches `v6.0.0` with worldgen, dimensions and structures, and builds blocks, items and creatures after (work-plan.md C1–C3, RB-22). Your bar is every 1.21.1 feature on 26.3. Does the switch wait for phase 3 (about 33 packages), or launch with phases 0–2 and ship content in slices after?
2. **Waiting mods: wait or build.** No 26.3 build today for YUNG's twelve, When Dungeons Arise and Seven Seas, dcintegration, and nine smaller structure packs. For each family: wait for the author (not delivered meanwhile), or build our own now (YUNG's-style overhauls about 4 packages, mega-dungeons about 3, chat bridge in discord-sync about 1)? If we build and the mod later ships, which wins?
3. **Cut mods: rebuild or let go.** The register cuts these with "nothing" as the replacement: Chipped, Macaw's Roofs, Universal Sawmill, Amendments, Creeper Overhaul, Guard Villagers, Dungeons Reborn, 3D Placeable Food, Fabric Seasons, BlueMap sign markers. Which come back as our own?
4. **Seasons.** P7 recommends accepting the loss. A server-side season (crop growth, snow, temperature) is about 1.5 packages; grass and leaf colours cannot change without a client.
5. **Better Combat** cannot be server-side (its animations are client-side). Accept the loss?
6. **Biome breadth.** 1.21.1 had roughly 350 modded biomes; 26.3 has 82 of ours and 67 vanilla. How many more of ours: about 60 (5 packages), or another number?
7. **Creatures and the server resource pack.** New creatures and blocks look right only to clients that accept the server resource pack (Bedrock through Geyser mappings). Should the pack be required on join (vanilla's "require resource pack"), so nobody sees stand-ins?
8. **Now-available mods.** Approve adding, through the dependency checklist and join gate: Friends & Foes with its Polymer patch, Explorify, DeCubed Dungeons, Repurposed Structures, Trek, Hopo Better Mineshaft, Server-Side Waystones, Sit!, Ender Dragon Destroys End Island, Chunky Border (or drop `/mc border`'s `chunky border add` instead).
9. **Portal ambience.** 1.21.1 played the far side's biome sound at a portal; it went with the immersive package (RB-03). Bring it back (a quarter package)?
10. **Already waiting on you** (HANDOFF State): the fifteen gameplay choices D1–D15, the overworld's two variants, the Batch 1–3 look-and-feel questions, the Bedrock phone test, `FLOODGATE_KEY_B64`, shaders on a GPU, Discord token checks, autopause.
