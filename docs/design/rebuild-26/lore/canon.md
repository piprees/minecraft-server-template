# Canon

Everything the 82 dimension descriptions (`description` in `config/custom-dimensions/dimensions/<slug>.json`, carried unchanged into `mods/custom-dimensions-26/definitions/<slug>.json`) say or directly imply about the world's past. The descriptions are the only source; there is no other lore text.

**These names are ours, for design reference only. None of them appears in game** (RB-43): no book, sign, map, item name or lore line names a people, a dimension, an era or a person. In game the same things are told by banners, materials, arrangements, remains and part-built frames.

`paradise_lost` is `the_aviary` in the 26.3 definitions; quotes give the 1.21.1 slug, entries give both.

Status: **canon** is stated in a description or follows directly from one. **Proposed** (marked `X`) is our connective tissue; it is few, labelled, and never contradicts a quote.

## The worlds that reach through the frame

Fifteen descriptions say the world takes ground beside its portal ("It takes through the portal too", the_buried_age; "what it reaches near the portal it takes", the_catalyst_maw; also the_blighted_maw, the_crimson_nexus, the_crucible, the_end_citadel, the_forged_depths, the_fractured_halls, the_frozen_strait, the_pillared_void, the_sculked_beyond, the_souldrift, the_teal_corruption, the_wailing_narrows, the_weeping_vault). This is canon for RB-41: a broken-portal study for one of these worlds already shows its ground taking hold. Six others turn a fall or a fire into a return ("it puts you back where you came from", the_fungal_lanterns; the_blighted_maw, the_starwell, the_slatemouth, the_red_monument "It declines them", the_lost_outpost's collapsing gate).

## Chronology

Relative, deep time to now. Each step cites its entries; order inside a step is not fixed by the text.

1. **Before anyone.** The growing (E1, P10, P11): the grown towers and vaults, the oldest tundra, the old fungus, the first forests and pools.
2. **The endermen's first works.** The pillars for the dragon, then the tiled expanse (E2, P17). The red monument and the starwell belong to the endermen's void worlds; their place in the order is not stated (X1).
3. **The ancient cities.** Built by endermen in the deep dark, left in the migration (E3, E4). The End's cities come after (the_end).
4. **The piglin ages.** An older civilisation lost to lava (E5); then the empire at its height (E6), its capital, sanctum, foundries and keep. The keep was "fortified against something that came from below" (P19).
5. **The war.** Piglins against endermen, neither winning (E7). The sculk feeds on it (P12). Ships crash, giants fall.
6. **After the war.** Survivors retreat, pacifists hide, the dead are quarantined and mourned (E8); the war-fed sculk is studied and escapes (E9); the flats keep running with no one at them (M21).
7. **The villagers' age.** Villagers settle the plains (P1) and plant the gardens round the oldest gate (L2); endermen teach them (E10); the witches split off (P8, P9). Older dungeons already lie under their land (E20).
8. **The arrival and the joint age.** Humans come through the stone gate (E11, L1); joint settlements, kingdoms, roads, quarries, the frontier (E12, E13).
9. **The waking.** Humans dig into the ancient cities and the sculk wakes (E14).
10. **The falling away.** A kingdom collapses and its soldiers stay (E15); settlements burn, empty or sink (E16); crossings fail (E17).
11. **Long after (where players arrive).** Everything above is over (RB-44). What remains is still going on its own: the bleed creeping (E18), the void winning (E19), the blight and the catalyst still feeding, the citadel still kept and the keeps "still building" (the_fractured_halls). These are processes that outlived the people who started them, not events in progress.

## Proposed connective tissue

Few, and each one fills a gap the text leaves open without contradicting it.

| Id | Proposal | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X1 | The red monument, the starwell and the tiled expanse are among the "older void dimensions" the endermen left | the_end names those worlds but not which they are; these three are the endermen's and lie outside the End |
| X2 | "Something that came from below" the blackstone keep (P19) is the sculk, risen long before the humans woke it again | the keep is piglin and old; the war-fed sculk (E9) and the ancient-city sculk (E14) then read as one power surfacing twice |
| X3 | The kingdom that fell (E15) is one of the sun kingdoms' neighbours, not the sun kingdoms themselves | the_sun_kingdoms "still stands"; its bandits (P13) are the lost outpost's descendants |
| X4 | The greatsword (M9) is one of several left by the same unseen hand in the Nether's quiet places (the_fungal_lanterns, the_basalt_spires, the_teal_corruption carry `mns:sword`) | gives a wordless recurring object with no explanation, which is the point |
| X5 | The older dungeons under settled land (E20) are outliers of the ancient cities' builders, in the same deepslate | ties E20 to E3 by material only |

## Tensions in the text

| Where | Tension | Reading used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the_tiled_expanse / the_pillared_void | "The endermen's first construction" against "the endermen's oldest construction, predating even the tiled expanse" | The pillars are older; the tiled expanse is the first thing they built to a plan. The chronology puts the pillars first |
| overworld / the_underdark | The ancient cities "predate everyone" against "Ancient cities built by the endermen" | "Everyone" means the villagers and humans who settled the overworld |
| the_boneyard / the_sculked_beyond | The sculk crept in and fed on the war, against the humans who "triggered this" | Two outbreaks of one power (X2) |
| the_end / the_starwell / the_end_citadel | The endermen moved on and left, yet the starwell is "The endermen's last gift" and the citadel "is still maintained" | The starwell is the last thing they gave before leaving their older worlds; the citadel's upkeep is a process that outlived them (Chronology 11) |
| Present-tense descriptions / RB-44 | Many descriptions speak of things still happening ("still feeding", "still building", "still spreading") | Processes left running, not people acting; no living faction is met at work |
| the_canvas | "No history, no ruins, no narrative", while its forced places include `minecraft:ancient_city` (dimensions.md) | The description wins for lore: the canvas carries no story and no lived-in layer |
| the_glacial_drift | "Nothing was ever built; nothing needs to be", while dimensions.md places a castle ruin, an igloo, towers, birdcages and a vault | Only grown things (towers, birdcages, vault as P10) fit; the castle ruin and the igloo contradict it |
| the_tidepools | "The wreck is the only evidence anyone else was ever here", while dimensions.md adds an ocean ruin, an ocean tower and buried treasure | One wreck only fits the text |
| the_fungal_lanterns / the_twisted_groves | "Even the piglins left these alone" and "The piglins avoided this place", while both place warped outposts | The outposts are someone else's (X4's unseen hand), never piglin-marked |
| the_nether, the_obsidian_sanctum | The descriptions name a mod ("Incendium's sanctum", "Incendium's cathedral dungeons") and the 26.3 pack places `nova_structures:sealing_halls` there | Lore reads it as "the sanctum"; the description text itself must not reach players (RB-43) |
