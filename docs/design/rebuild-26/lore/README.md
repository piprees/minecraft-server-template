# Lore

How the world's past is told through placement, for all 82 dimensions of the 26.3 rebuild. The only source is the dimensions' `description` fields; this folder turns them into placement requirements, not text for players.

- [canon.md](canon.md): every people, event, place, material and sign the descriptions allude to, quoted, with a relative chronology. Its names are for us; none appears in game.
- [stories.md](stories.md): the lived-in layer, the seven trails across dimensions and their wordless marks, and what is told only where it stands.
- [placement.md](placement.md): fitting each kind of place to its setting, the overworld as a history map, anchors and satellites, age, small worlds, what stays empty, and the kit gaps (SP2–SP4, DL1, LR1).

Principles: players arrive long after it all, and the world is a setting for their stories, not a plot (RB-44). Signs of use and time are everywhere a little; grand ruins are rare; most ground is empty (RB-42). Links are wordless: banners, trims, materials, arrangements, remains, part-built frames; nothing names a people, place, era or person (RB-43). Every place sits on its ground, where its builders would choose, of fitting materials, weathered to its age, with an approach (RB-45). Danger and reward rise together (RB-39); portals are found, not told (RB-40, RB-41).
