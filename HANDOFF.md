### Working rules learnt the hard way
- The machine (15 GB memory, a fixed disk allowance) has REBOOTED twice under load, both times with a Docker stack up and under 6 GB of memory and 2 GB of disk free: require 5 GB memory and 3 GB disk before a boot or a client launch, a progress line before each heavy step, a commit per finished unit. One full self-test or stack at a time; a worktree agent may run Gradle with `--max-workers=1` beside it.
- A worktree agent cannot work in the main checkout and its branch is local only: tell it to stay in its worktree, merge its branch when it reports, and never remove a worktree before checking `readlink /proc/<pid>/cwd` for processes in it. A stopped agent's unmerged branch is saved as a patch under `handoff/patches/`.
